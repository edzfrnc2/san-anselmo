About San Anselmo Publications, Inc.

San Anselmo Publications, Inc. is a Philippine-based independent publisher of books, journals, pamphlets, and audiobooks, known for its focus on local literature, arts, and socio-political themes. We actively participate in book fairs and collaborate with academic institutions to promote critical thinking and cultural appreciation.

Awardee: Gintong Aklat 2023 for Inspiration from Book Development Association of the Philippines.
