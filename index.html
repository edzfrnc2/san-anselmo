About Us

Preserving the Filipino Intellect

San Anselmo Publications, Inc. is a Philippine-based independent publisher of books, journals, pamphlets, and audiobooks. We are known for our focus on local literature, arts, and socio-political themes.

We actively participate in book fairs and collaborate with academic institutions to promote critical thinking and cultural appreciation across the nation.

Our Mission

San Anselmo Publications, Inc. was founded on the belief that independent publishing is one of the most vital acts of cultural stewardship. We champion writers who speak truth to power, who excavate memory, who imagine better futures — and we give their words a home.

Through our flagship literary magazine Santelmo: Liwanag sa Dilim, our growing library of books and pamphlets, and our outreach programs, we work to ensure that Filipino literature reaches readers across every generation and walk of life.

Santelmo: Liwanag sa Dilim

Santelmo is San Anselmo's flagship literary magazine, released multiple times a year. Each issue is thematically curated — spanning poetry, fiction, essays, visual art, and political commentary — and features a constellation of established and emerging Filipino voices. Now in its 13th issue, Santelmo has become an indispensable record of contemporary Philippine literary culture.
